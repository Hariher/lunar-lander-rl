How it works

Nobody programmed this landing. A small neural network flew the rocket thousands of times in Gymnasium's Lunar Lander, scored each attempt, and slowly nudged its own weights toward whatever earned more points. Every press of LAND replays a real episode flown by the trained agent.

8
Numbers in

Position, velocity, tilt, spin and two leg-contact sensors, 50 times a second. It never sees the picture.

4
Actions out

Do nothing, fire the left thruster, fire the main engine, or fire the right thruster.

64×64
Policy network

A two-layer MLP from Stable-Baselines3, trained with PPO for 1M steps on a laptop CPU.

95%
Episodes landed

38 of 40 test episodes landed safely. A score of 200 or more counts as solved.

Reward shaping. The agent learns exactly what it is scored on. Paying +1 for every frame spent upright and slow taught it to hover forever instead of landing (reward hacking). The fix: reward getting better between frames, not sitting in a nice spot, and charge a small fee for every frame so waiting costs something.

What you're watching. The physics, the trained policy and the touchdown are real. The visuals and sound are drawn and synthesized on top, frame by frame from the logged episode: engine on or off, thruster pulses, altitude, leg deploy and contact.
